RELIC FRONTIER v0.7.0 - October 13, 2026

START THE UPDATED GAME
1. Stop an older running game with Ctrl+C in its launcher window.
2. Open Start_Game.cmd inside the extracted game folder.
3. Confirm the browser says v0.7.0. Refresh with Ctrl+F5 if needed.

This shared repository includes the authored world, source and built interface.
Personal character saves and backup ZIPs are not uploaded. Before updating an
existing installation, stop the game and back up the complete folder, including
your own saves. Keep each version's world and save together.

COMBAT
KILL is an alias for ATTACK. For example: kill beetle
KILL without a name, distinct creature names, darkness and targeting work
exactly as they do with ATTACK.

ENCHANTING
Examples:
  enchant Iron Sword
  enchant Chain Vest
  enchant left
  enchant right
  enchant armor
  cast enchanting Iron Sword

Weapons must be equipped in a hand. Armor must be worn or held. Inventory,
shop and ground items cannot be enchanted. If matching items occupy both
hands, choose ENCHANT LEFT or ENCHANT RIGHT.

EQUIP <armor> wears it (shields go in the left hand). HOLD <armor> holds it in
the left hand; HOLD <armor> RIGHT selects the right hand. Held body armor
does not provide protection until worn; held shields provide protection.
The inventory also offers a Hold left button for armor.

Successful enchanting temporarily adds damage to a weapon or protection to
armor. It affects that individual copy, not every item of the same type.
Re-enchanting replaces/refreshes the enchantment; it never stacks endlessly.
Timers continue in inventory, storage, on the ground, and while the game is
closed. Expiration removes the magic bonus and the active enchantment cue.
Sharpening is separate and keeps its own bonus and expiration time.

Equipment, inventory and LOOK show the bonus beside the regular statistic:
  Armor 10 (+3)
  Damage 5 (+3)
The parenthesized amount is the enchantment bonus. Actual combat uses the
total. Enchanted items are marked and have a glow description; wording varies
with strength. LOOK also reports the remaining duration.

ENCHANTING LEVELS (separate from character level)
Every valid attempt awards 1 enchanting XP, whether it succeeds or fails.
Invalid targets, insufficient gold, locked skills, combat and cooldowns award
nothing and spend nothing. SKILLS and the Skills panel show level/progress.

Default progression and rewards:
- Level 1: 50% success, +1 damage/armor, 5 minutes.
- 100 attempts to reach level 2; then 150 XP to level 3, 200 to level 4, etc.
- Each enchanting level adds 2 percentage points of success and 1 minute.
- Every 3 additional enchanting levels adds another point of damage/armor.
- Maximum enchanting level 20: 88% success, +7, 24 minutes.
- Level 20 requires 10,450 total XP: mastery is deliberately slow.

Existing restrictions/costs remain: character level 3 unlock, 15-second
cooldown, and 20 gold per valid attempt. Failed weapon enchantments can still
damage or destroy the weapon under your existing risk settings (10% chance
of destruction after failure; otherwise 1 damage lost). Failed armor attempts
leave the armor unchanged. Failed attempts still award practice XP.

The owner editor's Crafting & balance category controls the XP curve, level
cap, chance, strength, duration, cost and weapon risks. Skills controls
eligibility, character unlock level and cooldown. No JSON editing is needed.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
Old enchanting proficiency is retained as the same numeric amount of XP.
Previously permanent positive weapon enchantments become temporary on first
load, keeping their bonus for the duration appropriate to the saved skill.
Reloading does not restart that timer. Existing physical weapon damage is
retained. Old rank/growth configuration fields remain in world data for
preservation but are superseded by the new enchanting-level controls.
New saves (including individual enchanted armor) require v0.7.0 or later;
use your original backup to return to v0.6.0.

Your save is in saves/game.sqlite3 and authored content is in content/world.json.
Stop with Ctrl+C before moving or backing up the game. Closing the browser
does not stop the game or its automatic combat.
