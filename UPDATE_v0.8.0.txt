RELIC FRONTIER v0.8.0 — SPELL AND MAGIC ITEM LEVELS

PLAY
Open Start_Game.cmd in this folder. Stop any old game with Ctrl+C first.
Refresh the game and owner editor with Ctrl+F5.

SLOW, INDEPENDENT PROGRESSION
Fireball and Lightning start at spell level 1 and keep separate experience.
Each individual Elven Sword also starts at magic level 1 and keeps its own XP.
The owner can enable progression on other weapons, armor and custom spells.
Character level and eligibility still decide when a spell becomes available;
spell level decides its power after it is unlocked.

By default, each valid use gives 1 XP. Level 2 takes 100 uses, level 3 takes
250 total uses, and level 20 takes 10,450 total uses. Each later step needs
50 more uses than the previous step. The default maximum is level 20.

Spells learn from valid casts, including misses and dodges. Invalid targets,
locked spells, cooldown retries, stunned attempts and healing at full health
earn no XP. Magic weapons learn from attacks that use them, including misses.
Casting a spell does not train a held weapon. Magic armor and shields learn
when equipped for protection and struck. Body armor merely held in a hand
does not grant protection or defensive XP. Recall scrolls stay single-use.

Spell damage is direct damage on a hit, without weapon or character-level
multipliers. Fireball retains its 25% scorch chance; scorch damage starts at
1 per pulse and rises to 4. Hit, dodge and status resistance checks remain.
Weapon power is the weapon contribution to a normal attack, which retains
the existing class and character-level contributions.

EDIT EVERY LEVEL
Open the Owner editor at http://127.0.0.1:8765/admin.
1. Open Skills & spells and select Fireball, Lightning or another skill.
2. Under Core settings & existing content, open Spell levels.
3. Edit Damage and Total XP to reach for any level.
4. For damage-over-time spells, edit Effect damage / pulse. A blank entry
   uses the attached status effect's ordinary damage setting.
5. Adjust XP earned per valid use, or add/remove the final level as needed.
6. Click Save world changes.

Healing and knockout spell tables control healing HP or success chance.
Turn Spell progression on for new custom spells. Physical skills may keep
their existing damage multiplier. Enchanting has its own progression.

For equipment, open Items & equipment, select a weapon or armor, and open
Magic item levels. Enable progression and edit Damage or Armor at each level.
Copies share the same balance table but keep separate experience. The ordinary
item stat applies when progression is off; the level table applies when on.

Level 1 starts at 0 XP. Later thresholds must increase. Editing tables retains
stored XP, so changing thresholds can change an existing spell or item level.

INSPECTION AND SAVES
SKILLS and the Skills panel show spell level, progress and current power.
LOOK and inventory/equipment panels show item level and progress. Purchased,
found, equipped, dropped and stored magic items retain individual identities.
Existing magical gear starts at level 1 on its first updated load.

Temporary enchantments remain separate: Damage 11 (+1) means 11 base damage
plus a temporary +1 enchantment. Expiration does not erase item training.
Enchanting keeps its existing adjustable progression, costs and failure risks.
Personal saves and owner settings stay local. Keep an updated save with the
updated code, and stop the game before making a backup.
