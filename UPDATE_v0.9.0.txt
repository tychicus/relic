RELIC FRONTIER v0.9.0 - MECHANICS AND CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT
Mechanics update, October 14, 2026

START AND PRESERVE
The connected development folder has been updated directly. Start_Game.cmd opens
it on port 8765 and continues your existing character. Stop any older launcher
first, and refresh the browser with Ctrl+F5 after launching the new version.

Try_Relic_Frontier_Update.cmd in this chat's outputs folder starts a fresh,
separate preview on port 8877. Each launch copies the current world into its own
preview-sessions folder and uses a separate character database. Owner changes in
the preview affect only that copied world. Preview sessions are retained.
Your original save and content history have not been modified by testing.
A complete pre-update backup is also in the chat's outputs folder.

CHARACTER LEVELS AND ABILITY CHOICES
Characters can reach level 100. The first eight XP thresholds are preserved;
level 10 requires 15,000 total XP and level 100 requires 6,697,500.
Each new character begins with ONE learned combat ability:
  Warrior: Swordplay, passive training for automatic weapon attacks.
  Mage: Fireball.
  Rogue: Backstab, a stronger physical strike.
ATTACK / KILL still starts automatic basic combat for all classes.

One learning point is earned at levels 5, 10, 15 and every fifth level. Points can be
saved. LEARN lists choices; LEARN KICK or the learning panel spends points to
learn an eligible ability. LEARN on a known ability improves its chosen rank.
Most abilities have five ranks and each later rank adds 10% power. Each later
rank requires five more character levels beyond its previous requirement.
Swordplay rank 1 is the normal weapon attack; later ranks strengthen it.
Learning is blocked during combat. Rejected choices spend nothing.
SKILLS lists learned abilities. Reaching an unlock level does not grant an
ability automatically. Class and race restrictions still apply.

Default learning milestones (ordinary abilities can be studied across classes):
   1: Swordplay, Fireball, Backstab (only your class starter is free).
   4: Sharpening.
   5: Kick, Mend, Quick Strike.
   8: Knockout.
  10: Bash, Lightning, Hamstring; Enchanting for MAGES ONLY.
  15: Charge, Frostbolt.
  25: Power Strike, Cutthroat.
  30: Arcane Lance.
  50: Shattering Strike, Nerve Strike.
  60: Meteor.
  75: Executioner, Assassinate.
Most early abilities cost 1 point per rank. Lightning, Frostbolt, Arcane Lance,
Enchanting and level-25/50 techniques cost 2. Level-60/75 abilities cost 3.
Sharpening and Enchanting have one chosen rank; enchanting grows through use.
Spells currently target one enemy, including Meteor.

Rogues have 15% dodge and +15 percentage points to escape chance.
Health also grows with character level using the existing editable HP setting.

Class multipliers apply at every chosen rank and spell mastery level:
  Warrior: martial 100%; arcane 35%, twice the mana, half the practice;
    subtle/rogue abilities 75% power and practice.
  Mage: arcane 100%; martial 30% power, half the practice;
    subtle abilities 40% power, half the practice.
  Rogue: subtle 100%; martial 75% power and practice;
    arcane 50% power, 1.5 times mana, 65% practice.
Penalties also reduce attached skill-effect application chances. Weapon effects
retain their own chances. A warrior's spell remains weaker than the mage's at
matching development, even at the default rank/mastery caps. A highly trained
hybrid can outperform a completely inexperienced specialist.
Enchanting remains restricted to mages; cross-class learning does not bypass it.

LONG MASTERY AND MANA
Chosen ranks are separate from slow spell and individual equipment mastery.
Spells and magical equipment have 100 default mastery levels. Each has a separate
XP record, including each individual copy of magical gear. Level 2 requires
100 XP, level 20 requires 10,450 and level 100 requires 252,450. Every level
improves power. The owner can edit every XP threshold and damage/healing value.
Scrolls remain single-use and do not develop independent mastery.

Valid use earns base practice even on a miss. An enemy at or above the character's
level gives twice the base practice; three or more levels above gives three times.
Released creatures give base practice only. Invalid targets, cooldown retries,
full-health healing and insufficient mana give no practice. Out-of-combat healing
and enchanting use base practice. Class practice penalties apply to spells.
Magical weapons practice through attacks; all worn magical armor pieces and a
held magical shield practice when a hit reaches their protection.

Mana is paid on valid casts. Fireball starts at 6 MP, Lightning 9, Mend 7 and
Enchanting 8; class penalties and editable per-mastery-level costs apply.
Valid paid casts also slowly train mana capacity. Mana mastery has 100 levels
with the same slow XP curve, adding 2 maximum MP per mastery level by default.
Character-level growth and mastery growth both contribute to maximum mana.
No mana recovers during combat by default. Outside combat, class recovery rates
apply while the game runs. REST at the inn restores health and mana. Character
level gains refill both; the finishing spell still pays its cost. Reloading
preserves mana and grants no offline recovery or practice. MANA displays details.

EQUIPMENT SLOTS AND WEIGHT
Slots: helmet, chest, pants, bracers/arms, right hand and left hand/shield.
EQUIP <armor> chooses its correct body slot. Old 'armor' slots remain chest armor.
UNEQUIP HELMET / CHEST / PANTS / ARMS / LEFT / RIGHT returns gear to the bag.
One-handed weapons can be used with a shield from level 1, including warriors.
Two-handed weapons use the right-hand slot and reserve the left hand; remove
the shield or other offhand item first. Two shields cannot be equipped together.
Dual wielding two one-handed weapons retains its class/level restrictions,
35% offhand damage contribution and 10 percentage-point hit penalty.

All worn armor and a held shield add to defense; each incoming hit still deals
at least 1 damage if it connects. Holding ordinary body armor for enchanting
does not grant protection. Each armor piece can keep its own enchantment and
magic XP. The equipment panel shows total defense and empty/occupied slots.
Anthony's shop stocks example helmet, pants, bracers, a shield and a greatsword.

Every item has an editable weight in pounds. Inventory shows total weight and
capacity; WEIGHT reports it in the console. Worn gear, chest contents, cages and
all cage occupants count. Equipping or putting items into a carried container
does not reduce total weight. Gold is not counted. Existing bag slot limits
also remain in effect. Capacity grows with character level, separately by class.
Buying, picking up, capturing, harvesting and cooking cannot increase carried
weight beyond capacity. Rejections preserve gold, ingredients and ground items.
Loot too heavy to carry remains on the ground. Existing over-limit saves keep
all possessions and remain playable; drop, sell or store weight before acquiring
more. Heavy creatures may require dropping other gear or gaining levels to carry.

COMBAT AND FIXES
LOOK SELF and EQUIPMENT show every hand and armor slot. Empty slots are listed;
two-handed weapons explicitly occupy both hands. Item names no longer have
decorative wrapping brackets. Active enchanted names appear violet in the
console and item panels; enchantment bonuses and glow descriptions remain visible.

DEATH RECOVERY AND BANKING
Death returns you to the fountain with: "You blink awake from death feeling
strange and disoriented." Your body remains where you died with every carried
and equipped item and all carried gold. LOOK BODY, GET ALL FROM BODY, or
GET <item> FROM BODY recovers belongings; BODY <number> selects among several.
Recovered equipment goes into your bag. Equip it to free bag slots if necessary.
After 15 minutes of real time, the body and everything still inside disappear,
including container contents. Closing the game does not pause this deadline.
The owner can adjust body lifetime in Combat & progression. Existing bodies keep
their deadline. Disabling recoverable bodies restores the legacy loss settings.

Go SOUTH then WEST from the fountain to the Brass Badger Bank. Osric Tallywick
keeps character-specific accounts. BALANCE, DEPOSIT <amount or ALL>, and
WITHDRAW <amount or ALL> work inside the bank. Deposited gold is safe after death.
Bank availability is a room setting; the banker and dialogue use the NPC editor.

COMBAT TIMING
Manual combat skills share 2 seconds of recovery in addition to their own
cooldowns. Automatic attacks continue. Potions share a 10-second recovery;
full-health use does not consume a potion. Both timings are editable.
The standard 2-second shared pulse checks every player and engaged enemy together.
Each fighter's basic attack recovers for 4 seconds, longer when slowed. The owner
can adjust the pulse interval. Enemies focus the first player to start a fight;
when enemies initiate, each independently rolls among the players present. Targets
persist until they die or leave. The shared scheduler supports multiple players;
the current local game still has one player and no network sessions.
Named spell targets now select the
requested enemy, and scouting includes fixed residents before the first visit.
Skill validation rejects unlocks beyond the world's configured character cap.

ENEMY HEALING
Wounded enemies recover 2% of maximum HP (rounded up) every 5 seconds after
20 seconds out of combat. Healing pauses for every engaged or unconscious enemy,
never revives defeated enemies and never grants offline healing. A delayed game
clock tick restores only one installment. Fixed, regional and released creatures
all use this system. Combat & progression controls the defaults; Creatures can
use their own delay, interval and percentage. Set 0% to disable a creature type.

REGIONS, WANDERING AND WEATHER
Owner > Regions & weather contains each region's membership, enemy spawning
squares, weighted enemy bank, population cap, per-square cap, spawn delay/chance,
wandering toggle/interval, weather and fog visibility penalty.

Default regional populations:
  Town: no random regional enemies; the vampire remains a fixed special encounter.
  Coppergrass Road: up to 2 beetles; replacements every 60 seconds; moves every 30.
  Relic Caverns: up to 2 goblins in the mouth/tunnel; replacements every 90 seconds;
    moves every 45. The sentinel remains fixed in the vault.
  Coppergrass Prairie: up to 18 enemies from hare, wolf, boar, raider, lion, deer,
    turkey and a rare cave troll; replacements every 60 seconds; moves every 30.
All spawning regions allow up to 3 creatures in a square. Bank weights control
relative frequency, not guaranteed counts. New worlds seed the populations once.
An existing save adopts living room creatures with their identity, wounds and
unconscious state intact, then fills remaining capacity gradually.

Enemies move only through adjacent exits within their region's spawning squares.
They cannot enter safe squares or wander while engaged or unconscious. Unconscious
creatures still occupy population slots. Defeat or capture frees capacity after
the replacement delay. Looking/reloading does not produce extra spawn attempts.
Changing the bank affects new creatures; current creatures are preserved. Limits
cannot be lowered below the current population, nor occupied squares removed.

Choose all squares under region membership for weather, and only eligible unsafe
squares under enemy spawning. Those squares use the regional bank instead of their
old room spawn rules. The old authored rules remain available if regional management
is removed after its creatures are defeated/captured. Other fixed encounters remain.
Newly added rooms must be selected in the regional membership and spawning lists.

Weather is an owner-selected description: clear, mist, fog, rain, storm or snow.
The separate 0-100 visibility penalty controls scouting difficulty. The stronger
of regional fog and room fog applies along the sight path. All defaults are clear.
Weather is displayed on room entry and in the room panel. It does not damage players.

MULTIPLE COMBATANTS AND FUTURE MULTIPLAYER
Several enemies can occupy a square. Hostile enemies join the encounter; neutral
ones join when provoked. Each engaged creature can attack, and defeating or knocking
out your target does not end the fight while another opponent remains. Use TARGET
with a description or the target button to switch opponents. Duplicate creatures
receive distinct descriptions and IDs. The room panel shows level and fighting state.

The encounter module tracks lists of players and enemies, a target per participant,
and independent attack deadlines. Tests exercise two players against two enemies,
including retargeting when a participant dies or leaves. The local game still has
one saved player. Network sessions, a shared room registry, party/reward rules and
multi-user command resolution remain future multiplayer integration work.

EQUIPMENT REPAIR
Anthony's shop offers repairs. REPAIR lists damaged carried/equipped equipment and
prices; REPAIR <item name or ID>, REPAIR RIGHT, REPAIR LEFT or REPAIR ARMOR repairs
one item. A Repair button appears beside damaged gear when service is available.
Default price is 5 gold per lost damage/protection point, with no additional fee.
Repair removes physical damage penalties and preserves identity, magic XP, active
enchantments and sharpening. It does not reset or extend buff duration. Repair is
blocked during combat. Undamaged gear and rejected attempts cost nothing.
Destroyed weapons cannot be repaired. Existing enchanting destruction risk remains.

  Characters > Classes: starter, category power/mana/practice multipliers, HP,
    attacks, mana, recovery, dodge, escape, carrying capacity and level growth.
  Skills & spells: unlocks, eligibility, category, costs, ranks, cooldowns,
    mana costs and all mastery rows for XP, power and spell-effect damage.
  Items & equipment: item weight, armor slot, shield toggle, required hands,
    normal damage/protection and individual magic mastery tables.
  Creatures: challenge level, carrying weight, combat and reward properties.
  Combat & progression: character XP thresholds, attack pulses, recovery and enemy healing.
  Mana & mastery: reserve growth, recovery and challenge practice bonuses.
  Regions & weather: enemy banks, population, movement and visibility.
  Crafting & balance: weight limit enablement, repairs, enchanting, dual wielding.
  World > Rooms: Equipment repairs available here and room-level rules.
Save world changes applies the draft and keeps a prior-world backup. Changes to
already-equipped item slots and deletion of learned abilities are rejected.

OLD SAVE MIGRATION
Existing announced or practiced eligible abilities are retained at rank 1 and
past character levels grant their learning points. Existing character, spell,
equipment and enchanting XP are retained. New characters use the one-starter rule.
Old chest armor remains equipped; other body slots start empty. Regional enemies
are adopted without discarding existing wounds, identities or unconscious state.
The original personal save was tested through a disposable copy only.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
225 Python tests, both JavaScript helper suites and TypeScript/Vite production
build checks cover the local update. Controlled fights used 100 seeds per class
for five sample encounters, with legitimate learning choices, fresh spell mastery,
standard gear and no potions. All classes won the sampled early encounters;
a level-10 vampire remained dangerous at character level 4. This is a starting
balance pass, not exhaustive playtesting of every build through level 100.
Local HTTP checks cover the game, owner editor, built assets and new features.
Browser visual verification could not complete because the available browser
could not connect to the preview. Use the separate launcher for visual review.
Review branch: update/mechanics-v0.9.0. Merging into main is a separate step.
