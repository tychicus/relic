RELIC FRONTIER v0.2.0 — TESTING AND CUSTOMIZATION

Use START_HERE.txt for installation. Work in the NEW extracted folder, keeping
your previous game untouched as a backup. Automatic migration adds the new
save fields on startup. No character reset is required.

1. SHOPS AND INVENTORY
Visit Anthony's Supply and type BUY. Each grouped entry has its own line:
  (Healing potion) — 8 gold each
  [Iron sword] — 35 gold each
Duplicate entries show (x2), (x3), etc. Shop entries are an unlimited catalogue,
not finite stock. Bag limits still count individual ordinary items, even when
the display stacks them. Each cage occupies one bag slot, regardless of its
occupants. Uncaged creatures each occupy one slot. Equipped items are separate.

2. EXPLORE THE PRAIRIE
From Center Square: N, N, N reaches Coppergrass Meadow. Then W enters the new
prairie. It is a four-by-four area with connecting paths north/south/east/west.
Mountain cliffs border the four northern locations. Mountain travel is reserved
for a later expansion. LOOK at each location's landscape objects for details.

Prairie creatures include horned hares, wolves, boars, raiders, and ridge lions.
Some attack on sight. Bring potions; your original vampire and other enemy
stats were not weakened. The cave troll has 220 HP and damage of 18–30 before
armor, making it a serious encounter even for an equipped level 6–8 character.
Enemy difficulty here comes from its stats; enemies do not have player levels.

Cave troll entry rolls:
- Each northern foothill room has a 5% chance on an eligible entry.
- Each roll, successful OR unsuccessful, starts that room's 300-second timer.
- LOOK, browser refresh, polling, and standing still never make extra rolls.
- Leaving/reentering before the timer expires does not make another roll.
- A spawned troll stays until killed or captured; reentry cannot duplicate it.
- A killed/captured troll also observes its 300-second respawn delay. The next
  eligible ENTRY is required; it will not respawn while you stand there.
- Each room has its own timer. Timers persist through restarts.

Owner editor > Rooms > choose one of:
  Western Scree Edge / Thunderstone Foothill / Coldwater Fan /
  Eastern Mountain Verge
Set Random enemy on entering this room, Chance on entry (%), and Time between
eligible rolls (seconds). Use 5 for 5%, not 0.05. Clear Resident enemy and Safe
town room before adding random encounters to another room.
For a quick test, set an unvisited northern room to 100%, save outside combat,
and enter it. Restore 5% afterward. A timer already running still applies.

3. VAMPIRE TREASURE
From Center Square: W enters the inn; DOWN reaches the vampire's dungeon.
KILL the vampire. Capturing it does not reveal its treasure. After a kill:
  GET RUSTY KEY
  LOOK BOX
  UNLOCK BOX
  GET DAWNFANG

The key and box cannot be seen before that kill. The rusty key must be in your
inventory, disappears on successful unlocking, and cannot be sold. A wrong
key or missing key opens nothing and consumes nothing. Dawnfang is a new +22
damage weapon. Its presence in the box can be changed in the owner editor.

The discovery is permanent. Vampire respawns do not hide it again, generate
another key, refill the box, or duplicate its weapon. Unlocking puts treasure
on the ground, so even a full bag does not lose it. Open boxes remain in place.
The first qualifying kill in this updated save reveals these new placements;
old kills are not retroactively counted.

4. ADD YOUR OWN HIDDEN ITEMS OR LOCKED BOXES
A. Create any needed item, weapon, and Key using Add something to your world.
B. Rooms > select a room > Add discoverable item / container.
C. Select Loose item or Locked container and give the placement a normal name.
D. Choose when it appears:
   - Player looks in the room: available when the room can be seen.
   - Player looks at an object: select an inspectable object in that room.
   - Room enemy is killed: select its resident or random encounter enemy.
E. For a loose item, select Item to discover. For a container, choose its
   Required key and check the Treasure inside. Add descriptions/messages.
F. Save world changes outside combat.

Each placement is one-time per saved game. For another independent treasure,
add another placement. Already discovered placements cannot be deleted or have
their type changed; this prevents save corruption. Editing contents of an
already opened box does not refill it. An unopened box uses your current
contents selection. Use different key items for locks that should not share
keys. The matching key is always consumed after successful opening.

5. CUSTOM ENEMY WORDS AND ACTIONS
Owner editor > Enemies > choose the creature. New fields:
- Battle cries / actions: one possible line per row.
- Chance of battle line per attack (%): 20 means 20% per enemy attack turn.
- Defeat words / actions: one random line when killed (not knocked out).
- Description when looking into its cage: shown on LOOK CAGE <number>.
- Possible captive words / actions: one random line per cage inspection.
Leave line lists empty for silence. These are descriptive text; they do not
change damage or grant items. The vampire and cave troll include examples.

6. CAPTURE AND PORTABLE CAGES
Buy a cage at Anthony's Supply:
  BUY IRON CAGE
It costs 45 gold and holds up to five creatures of ONE type. Edit its price or
capacity under Weapons & items > Iron cage. Occupied cages cannot be sold.
Empty cages may be sold for half their purchase price, rounded down.

Weaken an enemy and use KNOCKOUT. Default health requirement: 35% HP or less;
cave trolls require 25% or less. Knockout has a 12-second cooldown and 70% base
success chance, reduced by resistance:
  actual chance = base chance x (1 - resistance / 100)
For example, vampire resistance 35% gives 70% x 65% = 45.5% success.
The enemy must have Can be knocked out and captured = Yes. The mechanical
sentinel is not capturable. Attempts above the health threshold consume no
cooldown. A valid failed attempt consumes the cooldown and combat continues.

After success:
  GET VAMPIRE
  PUT VAMPIRE IN CAGE 1
  LOOK CAGE 1
  DROP CAGE 1
  GET CAGE 1

Use the enemy's actual name and the cage's actual displayed number. Each cage
has a unique number; it may not start at 1 if you have already captured things.
If multiple cages match, specify the number. Capturing another vampire and
putting it in the same cage increases its count. Different creature types need
different cages. Full/mismatched cages leave the creature in your inventory.

Successful knockout stops combat. Unconscious creatures do not wake up on a
timer in this version. If your bag is full, they remain in the room until GET
can succeed. Capturing gives no kill XP, gold, loot, or defeat-triggered
revelations. The original spawn may return after its normal respawn delay,
starting when you pick up the unconscious creature.

A cage can be carried full, dropped in a room, inspected there, and picked up
again with exactly the same occupants. Cages and captured creatures are
protected from random item loss on death. Their contents survive save/reload.
Captured creatures are collectibles for now: they do not fight for you, breed,
need food, or escape. Capacity is editable; lowering it below current occupancy
is rejected. A cage's description is also editable if you change its capacity.

Optional RELEASE CAGE 1 releases ONE occupant into the current room. It requires
a visible non-safe room with no other creature and no active fight. Aggressive
creatures attack again. Released creatures give no kill rewards or hidden
item revelations, preventing repeated reward farming. An emptied cage can
hold a different creature type.

Quick capture test without risking a kill:
In a separate testing copy, set a neutral creature's capture health to 100%,
resistance to 0%, and Skills > Knockout > Base success chance to 100%. Save
outside combat, then use KNOCKOUT on that creature at full health. Restore the
normal settings after testing. This avoids automatic attacks killing weak
creatures before you can knock them out.
