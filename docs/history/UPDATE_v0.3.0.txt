RELIC FRONTIER v0.3.0 — STORAGE, CREATURES & OWNER MAP

INSTALL
Stop your current game with Ctrl+C in its launcher and wait for Progress saved.
Keep the old folder as a backup. Extract this ZIP into a NEW folder, then open
Relic_Frontier/Start_Game.cmd. The launcher and room panel should say v0.3.0.
The launcher now prints the exact folder it is running from. If the browser
shows an older version, stop the old launcher, start this copy, and press Ctrl+F5.
No new Python packages are required. Ctrl+C and automatic saving are unchanged.

This update uses the world and saved character from the v0.2.0 ZIP, as requested.
Progress or edits made on your PC after that ZIP are not included. Do not
replace content/world.json with an older file: that would remove the new areas,
metadata and shop. Keep a complete backup when testing.

1. TEN-MINUTE DROPPED-ITEM CLEANUP
Ordinary items you DROP disappear after 600 seconds on the ground. Each dropped
copy has its own timer. GET cancels that timer; dropping it again starts a fresh
one. The timer persists across restarts and includes time while the game is off.
Expired items are removed on restart or the next game tick.

Keys, cages (empty or occupied), and storage chests NEVER expire. Chest contents
do not expire. An uncaged captured creature that you DROP is an ordinary timed
drop, so put valuable captives in a cage. Keys are still consumed when used in
matching locks, and selling a cage to Jake intentionally removes it.

Only player DROP actions start timers. Uncollected discovery treasure, overflow
loot, and ground items already in the old save are left alone because their
origin cannot reliably be identified as player drops. Pick up and drop an old
ordinary item if you want its cleanup timer to start.

2. PURCHASE AND USE A STORAGE CHEST
Anthony's Supply now sells a Storage chest for 60 gold. Edit its price/capacity
under Weapons & items. Default capacity: 30 items. Each item counts as one slot,
even when several identical items display as a stack. A full cage counts as one
stored item; the creatures inside stay in that cage.

  BUY STORAGE CHEST
  LOOK CHEST 1
  PUT POTION IN CHEST 1
  GET POTION FROM CHEST 1
  DROP CHEST 1
  GET CHEST 1

Use the actual chest number shown in your inventory. Numbering is shared with
individual cages and captures, so your first chest might be chest 3 or 7.
PUT/GET transfers one item at a time. The chest can be carried or on the ground
in your current visible room. Occupied chests can be moved and keep their items
through saving/restarting. A chest uses one bag slot, regardless of contents.
Chests are protected from random death item loss. Occupied chests cannot be sold;
empty chests sell at a normal shop for half price. Chests cannot go inside other
chests. Full storage or a full bag leaves items in their original place.

3. JAKE'S CRITTERS
From Anthony's Supply: WEST to Center Square, SOUTH to South Market Street,
then EAST into Jake's Critters. There is no direct doorway between the shops.
Return exits are present. Jake buys occupied cages only.

  SELL                shows offers for occupied cages in your inventory
  SELL CAGE 1         sells that cage and ALL its occupants

Payout = creature sale value x count. Three beetles worth 10 each pay 30 gold.
The cage adds no extra payment and is removed with its occupants. Empty cages,
loose creatures and other items are not bought here. Occupied cages are still
refused at ordinary shops. A value of zero means Jake will not buy that species.
You can also use the inventory Sell cage button when inside Jake's shop.

Owner editor > Enemies > choose creature > Sale value per captured creature.
Initial values: beetle 10, goblin 20, vampire 75, hare 12, wolf 25, boar 35,
raider 45, ridge lion 60, cave troll 150. Sentinel remains uncapturable/value 0.
The room option Buys occupied cages can give another shop Jake's trading rules.

4. ENEMY CHOICES, RESPAWN POOLS AND ROOM TIMERS
Owner editor > Rooms > choose room > Enemy spawning mode:

A. Repeat starting enemy: the previous behavior, with an optional room timer.
B. Choose from a respawn pool: choose a Starting enemy and add the possible next
   creatures, each with a positive weight. After death or capture, the room waits
   its timer and selects one creature. Equal weights mean equal chances. Weight
   3 is three times as likely as weight 1. One creature occupies the room at a
   time. An unconscious enemy must be picked up before its replacement timer
   starts. The new creature can be a different species.
C. Rare entry-roll encounter: keeps the cave-troll system. Chance/cooldown rolls
   happen on eligible entry only. A room cannot use both a pool and entry rolls.

Room respawn delay is in seconds. Blank uses the defeated/captured creature's
normal respawn time. With a pool but no Starting enemy, the room starts empty;
its first spawn waits the room timer (60 seconds if blank), beginning when the
room is first checked by the game. An empty pool makes no replacement enemies.
Timers include offline time; elapsed normal respawns appear when the room is
next evaluated. Entry encounters still require reentry, even after their timer.
The entry-roll cooldown and the post-defeat respawn delay must BOTH have elapsed.

Existing living enemies are retained if still allowed by the starting choice or
pool. Changing Starting enemy is not a reset button. Removing the current species
from all room choices clears that spawn. Timer edits affect future defeats and
captures; existing countdowns keep their deadlines. Finish combat before editing.
Safe rooms cannot have resident, pooled or random enemies. Defeat-triggered room
discoveries can target a creature in the pool as well as the starting enemy.

respawn pool, set a 10-second timer, save, kill/capture the beetle, then wait.
Existing room behavior is unchanged until you configure a pool or room timer.

5. HIDDEN CREATURE CONVERSATIONS
Owner editor > Enemies > choose creature > Add secret phrase.
Set the exact phrase, the response/action text, and whether it starts an attack.
Add as many rules as you want. An optional default response handles unknown
phrases. Players are not shown the trigger list or rules in the game display.
This remains a local owner-editable game; the owner editor can inspect all rules.

  SAY HELLO TO VAMPIRE
  SAY YOU DON'T SCARE ME TO VAMPIRE
  SAY HELLO TO CAGE 1

Matching ignores case and repeated spaces, but otherwise uses the full phrase,
including punctuation. The vampire has example rules for hello (peaceful) and
you don't scare me (starts combat). Neutral creatures can become hostile from a
rule. A response with Attack = No does not stop an already active fight.
Caged creatures respond, but their cages always prevent them from attacking.
Specify a cage number to address a captive when a free creature is also present.
You must be able to see a free creature; unconscious creatures cannot converse.

6. REGIONS, PERMANENT ROOM REFERENCES AND OWNER MAP
Owner editor > Owner map. All rooms are included, including hidden ones.
Rooms have an editable Region name and a permanent reference such as TOWN-001
or PRA-004. Room names and regions may change; existing codes stay fixed.
The normal game room panel also displays its code and region for easy reference.

- Click a room on the map, or its button in the room list, to open its editor.
- Search by name, code, or region; optionally filter to one region.
- Scroll the map for larger areas. Elevations appear as separate labeled panels.
- Arrows indicate travel directions. Dashed links connect elevations.
- Export labeled map (SVG) downloads the current filtered view, readable in a
  browser and suitable for sending with your game ZIP.
- Export room directory (JSON) downloads all room names, codes, regions,
  positions and exits in a compact reference file.
- Exports reflect the editor draft; save your changes before exporting a final map.

TO ADD A SPACE NEXT TO AN EXISTING ROOM
Click the existing room > Add connected room > choose a direction > enter a
name and description > Create room and both exits > Save world changes.

The editor places the new room one step in that direction, gives it the same
region and a new permanent code, and adds both travel directions automatically.
UP and DOWN create a new elevation. New rooms start with no enemy; configure
spawning, shops, discoveries and objects in their room settings afterward.

An existing exit will NOT be overwritten. Open existing exit takes you to its
room. If another room already occupies the destination position, creation is
refused; use the normal exit fields to connect those existing rooms. Manual
exit editing does not move rooms or automatically add reverse exits. The map
will draw the travel connections you actually configured. Two rooms cannot be
saved at the same coordinate on the same elevation.

Generic Add room / place still creates an unconnected room at a free map position
under Unsorted. Use Add connected room for normal world expansion. Regions are
labels you can type, not player travel restrictions. Tell me a room code when
requesting future expansions, and include your latest ZIP or exported directory.

7. SAVE COMPATIBILITY / SCOPE
Your v0.2.0 character and existing content are retained. New state fields migrate
automatically on startup. Tests run in isolated temporary copies. Keep the whole
folder when backing up; older versions cannot safely read these new containers.
For your son's fresh game: copy the folder, stop the game, delete saves ONLY in
his copy, and start it. World customizations remain; character progress resets.

No player-facing exploration map or multiplayer has been added. No shortcut
changes were made. Secret reactions are text plus optional attack, not scripted
quests, item gifts, or spells. These can be extended later.
